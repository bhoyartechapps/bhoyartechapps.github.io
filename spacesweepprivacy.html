SpaceSweep Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

SpaceSweep helps you free up space on your iPhone by finding duplicate and similar photos, screenshots, large photos and videos, and duplicate contacts. It is designed so your data stays on your iPhone.

The short version

  • SpaceSweep does not collect, store or share any personal data on servers.
  • Your photos, videos and contacts are analyzed on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Photos and videos

With your permission, SpaceSweep reads your photo library to find items you may want to remove or compress. Analysis (for example, comparing photos, measuring file sizes and ranking the best shot) happens entirely on your iPhone. To make later scans faster, SpaceSweep keeps fingerprints of your photos (hashes and file sizes, never the photos themselves) on your iPhone. You can clear them in Settings > Clear Scan Cache.

Nothing is deleted without your confirmation, and iOS asks you to confirm again. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they can be recovered for 30 days. Compressed copies are saved to your library only when you choose to save them.

Contacts

With your permission, SpaceSweep reads your contacts to find duplicates and incomplete entries. Before every merge or deletion, a backup of the affected contacts is saved on your iPhone so you can restore them from within the app. Your contacts never leave your iPhone.

Private Vault

Items you add to the Private Vault are encrypted and stored only on your iPhone. Your PIN is never stored in readable form. Face ID, if you turn it on, is handled by iOS; SpaceSweep never receives your biometric data. If you delete the app, the vault and its contents are deleted with it.

Widgets and charging display

Widgets show storage totals and the totals from your last scan (counts and sizes only). This summary is shared between the app and its widgets on your iPhone. The charging display reads your battery level from iOS and does not store it.

Data we collect

None. SpaceSweep has no account system, no servers that receive your data, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Diagnostic logs on your device never include photo details, contact names, phone numbers, email addresses or vault content.

Children

SpaceSweep does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
