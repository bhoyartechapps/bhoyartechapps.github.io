Help with SpaceSweep: Photo Cleaner

Contact

Email appsupport.bhavesh@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. You can find SpaceSweep's version in Settings > About inside the app.

Common questions

I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted photos and videos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days. Contacts are backed up before every merge or deletion; restore them in SpaceSweep under Contacts > Backups.

Why didn't my storage go down after cleaning up?

iOS frees the space once items are removed from Recently Deleted. Open Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted to empty it.

Why are some photos or videos missing from the results?

Items stored only in iCloud (with Optimize iPhone Storage on) can't be measured or compressed until they're downloaded to your iPhone. If you gave SpaceSweep access to only some photos, it can only see those; change this in Settings > SpaceSweep > Photos.

Why can't I compress a Live Photo?

A compressed copy is a still image, so the motion would be lost. SpaceSweep leaves Live Photos out to protect them.

Can SpaceSweep clear System Data or other apps' caches?

No. iOS doesn't let any app clear System Data or other apps' storage. SpaceSweep focuses on what it can clean safely: your photos, videos and contacts.

I forgot my Private Vault PIN.

On the vault's lock screen, tap Forgot PIN and confirm with Face ID or your iPhone passcode to set a new PIN.

How do I show the charging display automatically?

In the Shortcuts app, create a personal automation for "When Charger Is Connected" and add SpaceSweep's "Open Charging Display" action.
